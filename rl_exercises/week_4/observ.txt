- hidden_dim = 128

Observation:
Increasing network depth did not improve performance. The training was stable but slower and achieved lower rewards (~220), indicating deeper networks are not necessary for this task.


Overall:
- Larger networks improve learning speed but can reduce stability.
- Larger replay buffers and batch sizes lead to smoother training.
- Deeper networks are not always beneficial for simple environments like CartPole.
