Level 2: PPO

PPO learned significantly faster than Actor-Critic on LunarLander-v3. Both PPO variants reached positive returns much earlier and maintained higher average performance throughout training.

Two PPO enhancements were implemented:
1. Learning-rate annealing
2. KL early stopping

The enhanced PPO variant showed slightly more stable learning behavior, while achieving similar final returns to vanilla PPO. Learning-rate annealing reduced update sizes near the end of training, and KL early stopping prevented overly large policy updates.

Overall, PPO outperformed Actor-Critic, and the enhancements provided a modest improvement in training stability.
